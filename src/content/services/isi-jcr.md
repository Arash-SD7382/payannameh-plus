An ISI or JCR article matters for university applications, PhD interviews, a stronger research profile and university requirements. Our team in your field stays with you from choosing a topic to submission and follow-up.

## What we do

- Choose a topic that fits your field and target journals
- Write the article in precise academic language
- Help you pick a suitable journal and submit the paper
- Respond to reviewers and make the required revisions
- On request, check similarity with iThenticate and Turnitin and provide an official Similarity Report

## Good to know

Final acceptance is decided by the journal and its reviewers. We provide the best writing quality and careful follow-up.

## Who it is for

Applicants, Master's and PhD students and researchers who need articles in reputable international journals.
