The data part of a research project or thesis often takes the most time. We support you here, from preparing the data to interpreting the results.

## What we do

- Clean and prepare data
- Run descriptive and statistical analysis
- Train and evaluate machine learning models
- Draw charts and interpret the results
- Deliver a report and project files

## Who it is for

Students and researchers who need data analysis or modelling for a thesis, article or project.
