Once your thesis or dissertation is finished, one or more academic articles can be drawn from it to strengthen your research profile. This is more than summarising: the text must be rebuilt into the shape and logic of an article.

## What we do

- Choose the parts of your thesis that suit an article
- Rewrite the abstract, introduction, methods and results in article form
- Adapt the text to your target journal or conference
- On request, check similarity

## Who it is for

Graduates and students who have a finished thesis or dissertation and want to publish articles from its results.
