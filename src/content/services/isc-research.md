A scientific-research or ISC article is often required for defence, promotion and a research profile. Every journal has its own format and rules, and we prepare your work to match them.

## What we do

- Choose a suitable journal for your field and topic
- Write the abstract, introduction, methods, results and conclusion
- Format the paper to the journal's author guidelines
- Set up references and respond to reviewers

## Who it is for

Master's and PhD students and faculty members who need articles in national scientific-research and ISC journals.
