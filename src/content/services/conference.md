A conference paper is a fast way to present your research and get feedback. We support you in choosing the event, writing the paper and preparing your presentation.

## What we do

- Suggest conferences and symposia that suit your topic
- Write the full paper or extended abstract in the event's template
- Design PowerPoint slides for your presentation
- Help with submission and follow-up

## Who it is for

Students and researchers who want to present their work at national or international conferences.
