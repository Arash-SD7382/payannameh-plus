Good research work must be clean, consistent and in the right format. We edit and format your text and check it for textual similarity.

## What we do

- Edit and proofread the text
- Format to your university's guidelines or the journal's author guide
- Rewrite passages to reduce textual similarity
- On request, check similarity with iThenticate and Turnitin and provide a report

## Who it is for

Students and researchers who have a draft and want to improve it before handing it in or submitting to a journal.
