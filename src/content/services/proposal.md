A proposal is the first step of any research project, and its quality sets the course of the work. We help turn your idea into a clear proposal you can defend.

## What we do

- State the problem and why the research matters
- Set aims, questions or hypotheses
- Review the existing research
- Design the methodology and timeline

## Who it is for

Students who need a proposal to register a thesis topic or a project.
